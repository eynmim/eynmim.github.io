Searches with no title at all

Ask everyone the same four questions

  1. What does a normal week actually look like, not the job description?
  2. If you were 25 today with what you know, would you pick this area again? Why?
  3. What do people entering this area usually complain about after a year?
  4. Bigger or smaller in five years, and why?
After each call, five lines in exploration.md (outside the repo): area, name, date, your energy after the call (up / flat / down), one thing you didn’t know, one thing that scared you, talk again yes/no. After 16 conversations, read the energy column first.
Small companies (under ~50 people): message the CTO / Responsabile Tecnico / Titolare directly. In a 20-person electronics firm they personally did the board, the firmware and the certification.
Narrow by Location (Italy / Germany / Norway…), Current company and School in LinkedIn’s own filter bar once the results open.
